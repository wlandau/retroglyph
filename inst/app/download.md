Do not trust the reconstructed data until you have reviewed all the previous panels. Example failure modes:

1. The isolated Kaplan-Meier curves disagree with those in the original image.
2. One or more occluded curves may be poorly traced or incomplete.
3. The x and y axis labels may be wrong, in which case the reconstructed data will be on the wrong scale.
4. The numbers at risk read from the original image do not match the original. Check them against the source, however many the model read.
5. The step function in a Kaplan-Meier data series has too few jumps for the algorithm to reconstruct the data accurately.

If the reconstructed image matches the original, and the numbers at risk and statistics agree with the source publication, then the reconstructed data is likely to be accurate enough for downstream analyses.
Use your judgement based on the results shown in the review tabs in this app.
